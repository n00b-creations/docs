# Stage 27 — End-to-End Autonomous Revenue Loop

The production revenue loop becomes:

```text
Signal → Enrich → Score → Predict → Prioritize → NBA → Policy → Route → Execute → Outcome → Revenue → Attribution → Learning
```

## Required measurements
- signal-to-qualified-lead rate
- qualification-to-appointment rate
- appointment show rate
- opportunity conversion
- close rate
- revenue per lead
- cost per qualified lead
- cost per appointment
- expected vs realized revenue
- response latency
- workflow/agent cost

## Learning
Capture prediction, decision, action, outcome, attribution, and calibration records. Model updates are versioned. Learning cannot modify authorization, consent, suppression, security, tenant isolation, or financial policy.

## Experimentation
Support controlled experiments with explicit cohorts, exposure logging, guardrails, stopping rules, attribution, and rollback.
