# Stage 30 — Production Launch & Commercial Operations

Launch through controlled tenant waves.

## Go-live gates
- security sign-off
- migration validation
- backup/restore verification
- observability verified
- incident response tested
- support procedures ready
- billing/entitlements verified
- connector health verified
- rollback tested
- tenant isolation verified

## Launch waves
Internal → design partners → early commercial tenants → controlled expansion → general availability.

## Customer operations
Provisioning, onboarding, activation, support, incident communications, SLA tracking, usage metering, billing reconciliation, renewal/expansion workflows, and customer health.

## Rollback
Every wave has an explicit stop condition, owner, rollback point, and evidence package.
