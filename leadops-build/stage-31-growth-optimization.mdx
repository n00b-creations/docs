# Stage 31 — Growth, Optimization & Continuous Operations

After launch, the platform becomes a continuously measured operating system.

## Growth loop
```text
Acquire → Activate → Adopt → Retain → Expand → Refer
                         ↓
                    Measure
                         ↓
                   Experiment
                         ↓
                   Learn / Calibrate
                         ↺
```

## Optimize
Continuously evaluate lead quality, conversion, workflow efficiency, agent cost, connector performance, infrastructure cost, customer health, partner economics, capacity, forecast error, anomaly precision, and autonomous-operation outcomes.

## Governance
Optimization candidates remain subordinate to policy, authorization, consent/suppression, commercial authority, and tenant boundaries.

## Operating cadence
Daily: incidents, queues, failed executions, revenue signals.
Weekly: funnel, experiments, customer health, unit economics, capacity.
Monthly: forecasts, retention, expansion, partner economics, SLOs, security evidence.
Quarterly: architecture, disaster drills, policy review, model governance, pricing/product review.

## Final definition of done
The LeadOps platform is operationally complete when the system can acquire and qualify demand, execute governed revenue workflows, measure outcomes, support customers, operate at production scale, recover from failure, and continuously improve without autonomously expanding its authority.

**Operating principle:** intelligence can improve decisions; automation can improve execution; neither can redefine authorization.
