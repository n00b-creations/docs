# CYBER-OS v2.5

CYBER-OS v2.5 is a modular cyber operations platform organized around mission-specific workspaces, reusable widgets, plugins, AI-assisted workflows, and operational visibility.

## Workspace profiles

- Red Team — authorized security assessment and adversary-emulation workspace.
- Blue Team — SOC, detection, monitoring, and incident-response workspace.
- Purple Team — attack-vs-detection validation, ATT&CK coverage, and detection-gap analysis.
- DFIR — evidence, case management, forensic timelines, artifacts, and chain of custody.
- DevSecOps — code, CI/CD, containers, Kubernetes, IaC, and security engineering.
- Threat Intel — indicators, campaigns, ATT&CK relationships, and intelligence reporting.
- NOC — infrastructure, topology, availability, and fleet operations.
- Executive — high-level security posture and operational summaries.
- AI Operations — visual workflows, local AI assistants, knowledge retrieval, and approved automation.

## Shared platform capabilities

Dockable and resizable panels, multi-monitor layouts, drag-and-drop dashboards, themes, widget marketplace, asset inventory, fleet management, cluster monitoring, topology visualization, attack-path visualization, timeline/event correlation, plugin dependency graphs, notes, global search, command palette, and AI Copilot.

## Safety boundary

Tool catalogs and workflow interfaces are designed for authorized testing, defensive operations, training, and isolated lab environments. The prototype uses mock data and does not execute real-world offensive actions.
